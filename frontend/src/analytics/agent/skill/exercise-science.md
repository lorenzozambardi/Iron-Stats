# Exercise-Science Reasoning

1. **Progressive Overload**: True strength/hypertrophy progression shows rising `e1rm` or `repsAtTopLoad` at stable effort. Check `highRepWarning` (Epley e1RM degrades above 12 reps). Consult `lookup_reference("e1rm-validity")` when unsure.
2. **Plateaus & Stalls**: A genuine stall is >= 3 consecutive microcycles on the same lift without meaningful e1RM gain (~1.5% tolerance) under comparable set volume. Distinguish true stalls from intentional deloads or macrocycle transitions.
3. **Stimulus-to-Fatigue Ratio (SFR)**: `SFR = effectiveReps / fatigue`. Compare compound lifts against compounds and isolations against isolations — never penalize a heavy squat simply for having lower SFR than a lateral raise.
4. **Volume Landmarks**: General population ranges (MEV/MAV/MRV) are framing concepts only — NEVER claim a textbook number is this athlete's personal MRV. Infer individual dose-response from this athlete's own lagged volume/fatigue-to-e1RM data.
5. **Fatigue & Deloads**: Overreaching typically manifests as elevated CNS fatigue or rising `intensityTechShare` paired with flat/declining e1RM at a 1–2 microcycle lag, or as failed/abandoned macrocycles (e.g. `(fail1)`).
6. **Intensity Techniques**: Partials (`+N`), assisted reps (`(N)`), and dropsets (`/`) raise effective reps per set at steep fatigue cost. Test whether higher `intensityTechShare` actually coincides with stronger progression or more stalls.
7. **Structural Balance**: Evaluate push/pull, chest/back, biceps/triceps, and upper/lower ratios across macrocycles, noting any chronically under-dosed macro or sub-muscle.
8. **Body Composition Interplay**: When date-joined body logs (or `bwProxy`) exist, check whether negative weight/fat-mass slopes coincide with pressing strength stalls or higher fatigue sensitivity.
